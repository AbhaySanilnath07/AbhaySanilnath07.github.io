Learning note · 2026

Start small, then make it clear

The most useful first version of a project is often smaller than the idea in your head. A small version gives you something real to test, explain, and improve.

When I get stuck, I return to three questions: what should the user accomplish, what is the smallest useful interaction, and how will I know it works?

Project note · 2026

What I learned from a game loop

Interactive projects make timing visible. Keeping movement, drawing, input, and feedback in separate steps makes a game easier to reason about and easier to change.
